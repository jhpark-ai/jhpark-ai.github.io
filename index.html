Junghwan Park

I am an AI researcher at TelePIX working on computer vision and the learning problems underneath it, from implicit neural representations to few-shot adaptation and satellite perception. My main line of work asks how pretrained visual representations transfer and adapt to new tasks, and how far those methods carry into remote sensing and spaceflight.

I previously spent four years on neural surrogate modeling for semiconductor devices, working on knowledge transfer between devices and on sample-efficient learning from limited measurement data. Much of my current research revisits those problems in a visual setting. I am particularly interested in methods that adapt existing models efficiently and remain reliable outside the settings they were built for.

01

Research

Transfer & adaptation

How pretrained visual representations carry over to new tasks with little data or compute: few-shot and test-time adaptation, task arithmetic across models, and training-free segmentation on frozen features.

BOLT · Platonic Task Arithmetic · FROST

Neural representations & operators

Implicit neural representations and neural operators as compact function approximators: closed-form fitting, backpropagation-free compression, and physics-guided operators for scientific data.

Closed-Form INR · ELMZip · PIANO · FLAME

Onboard satellite AI

Perception that runs on the spacecraft itself: 6-DoF pose estimation, onboard compression, and vision-language models that summarize before downlink, across RGB, multispectral and hyperspectral imagery.

ASTRA · DRIFT · Summarize First, Download Later
